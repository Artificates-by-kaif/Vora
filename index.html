Vora Features

  • F1Text Mode

    Chat with Vora using text messages.

  • F2Listening Mode

    Vora can listen and respond. Press Enter to start listening, then press Enter again to submit.

  • F4Friend Mode

    Vora listens to everything you say and responds automatically. Vora can speak freely and talk like a human.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Extract the whole ZIP into a normal folder (not from inside the ZIP, not Program Files).
  2. Double-click Vora.exe. If Windows says "protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The program is not signed with a paid certificate.
  3. Paste a free AI key in the setup window. It stays on your PC.

Check your download

You need

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), internet, a microphone for voice, Google Chrome, and about 3 GB of free space.

Vora's mood is a simulation, not real feelings. Your messages go to the AI service whose key you use.
