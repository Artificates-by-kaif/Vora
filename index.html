What Vora can do

Say it or type it. Vora does the work on your PC.

Files and folders

Create folders anywhere on your PC and find any file or folder, fast.

Apps and websites

Open any app or website, or open YouTube and search for anything.

WhatsApp messages

Send a message to anyone on WhatsApp by voice or text.

Screenshots and recording

Take screenshots and record your screen, with system sound.

System controls

Change brightness and volume, manage notifications, and more.

Python projects

Create a Python project together with its folder in one command.

Ask other AIs

Send your question to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini without leaving Vora.

Speaks and listens

Vora talks back and understands your voice, in English and Roman Urdu.

Three ways to talk to Vora

Switch modes with a single key.

F1

Text Mode

Chat with Vora using text messages.

F2

Listening Mode

Press Enter to start listening, then Enter again to send what you said.

F4

Friend Mode

Vora listens all the time and answers by itself, talking freely like a person.

Start in three steps

Vora is portable, so nothing gets installed.

  1. Download the ZIP file with the button at the top.
  2. Extract the ZIP to any folder you like.
  3. Run Vora and press F1, F2 or F4 to begin.

Check your download

Vora can control your PC, so verify the file before you run it.

SHA256: shown after the first release

In PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\Vora-Portable-*.zip

You need: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), internet, a microphone for voice, Google Chrome, and about 3 GB of free space.

Vora's mood is a simulation, not real feelings. Your messages go to the AI service whose key you use.
